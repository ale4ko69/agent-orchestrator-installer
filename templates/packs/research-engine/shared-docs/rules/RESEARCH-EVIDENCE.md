# Research Evidence Rules

Use research engines to gather evidence, not to replace judgment.

## Required

- Keep source links.
- Prefer primary sources when technical or legal accuracy matters.
- Record the date of research for time-sensitive topics.
- Separate quoted facts from inference.
- Distill findings before adding them to durable project memory.

## Do Not

- Do not paste secrets or private project data into research tools.
- Do not treat answer summaries as authoritative without checking sources.
- Do not use stale research for current pricing, laws, APIs, standards, or product capabilities.
- Do not mix external research with local code impact analysis; use CodeGraph/source reads for code.
